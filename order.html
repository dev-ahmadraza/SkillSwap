GET TO KNOW US

About SkillSwap

A student-powered platform for learning, sharing, and growing.

Skills are better when shared.

SkillSwap is a student-focused platform where university students can offer their skills, discover services, learn from other students, and collaborate on academic and creative projects.

Contact Our Team
✦

Learn. Share. Grow.

Building a community where every skill has value.

The Problem We Solve

Students often struggle to find affordable services, reliable academic support, and opportunities to use their own talents. SkillSwap brings students together in one place to help solve these problems.

How SkillSwap Works

Students can browse services, buy help from other students, sell their own skills, offer tutoring, and exchange knowledge with their university community.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower students by making skill-sharing accessible, encouraging collaboration, and helping young people develop practical experience.
